Piano Keyboard

Two octaves, playable with touch, mouse or keyboard.

Turn your phone sideways to play both octaves.

How to play

Tap or click a key, or use your keyboard: Z through M plus S, D, G, H, J play the lower octave, and Q through U plus 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 play the upper octave, matching the hints shown on each key.
